About me

PASSENGER PROFILE

Hi! I'm Alexa Abanes, a first-year college student currently exploring the world of Programming. I'm still learning the basics of Java and discovering how coding works, one step at a time.

As a beginner, I know that learning something new comes with challenges, but I'm willing to improve and become more confident in what I do. Outside the classroom, I enjoy traveling and discovering new places, which inspired me to give my portfolio an airline ticket-inspired design. Just like traveling, I see Programming as a journey where every experience teaches me something new.

MY PROGRESS SO FAR

My learning journey

FLIGHT JOURNAL

My journey in Programming has been a mix of excitement, confusion, challenges, and small achievements. Learning Java introduced me to the basics of coding, and along the way, I realized that understanding a problem and knowing how to turn it into code can be challenging.

Sometimes, I struggle with the logic, remembering the correct syntax, and figuring out errors in my programs. However, these challenges remind me that learning takes patience and practice. Through our activities and group tasks, I also learned the importance of communication, sharing ideas, and cooperating with others.

I know I still have a lot to learn, but my goal is to become more confident in coding independently, improve my problem-solving skills, and eventually create my own programs and projects.
